# Community Feature Development Plan

## Overview
This document outlines the development plan for the community features, designed to:
- Enable deep engagement with interests
- Foster genuine connections
- Support both open and private communities
- Facilitate real-world interactions
- Enable cross-pollination of ideas

## Core Philosophy
- Communities form organically around interests
- Depth over breadth in engagement
- Real-world connection over online-only interaction
- Natural learning through immersion
- Cross-pollination between different interests
